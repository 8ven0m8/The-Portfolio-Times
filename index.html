SmallLLM – Pathology Language Model

Built a GPT-2 style decoder-only transformer in PyTorch (10.8M and 25M params) with self-attention, residual connections, and layer norm from scratch. Curated 52M characters from 13 pathology textbooks, cleaned OCR artifacts, and published dataset on HuggingFace (with 59 public downloads). Achieved val loss 0.8794 on the 25M model.
